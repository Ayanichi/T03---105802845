What we cover

Energy consumption made simple

01

Energy use

Appliance electricity consumption is measured in watts (W) the rate of power draw and kilowatt-hours (kWh), which reflect how much energy is used over time. A higher wattage doesn't always mean higher running costs, how often and how long an appliance is used matters just as much.

02

Australian market

Drawing on a dataset of television listings sold in Australia, this project looks at the mix of screen technologies, screen sizes, brands and power ratings currently on the market, and how they relate to customer star ratings.

03

Televisions

LED-backlit LCD dominates the market, 55"-75" is the most common size range, and screen size is the strongest driver of power use. Head to the Televisions page for the full breakdown.

Project note

Bigger Screens use up More Energy, Cept Other Factors also Matter

Screen size is the single biggest factor in a television's power use, but screen technology and brand also play a role: standard LCD panels run cooler than LED-backlit or OLED sets on average, even though LED (LCD) is by far the most common technology sold in Australia.
