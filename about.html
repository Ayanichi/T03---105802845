About the Project

About Us

Austravision is a student project focused on making appliance energy information easier to understand.

Our purpose

This website presents research into television energy consumption of the 2015 Australian market, aiming to make appliance energy data easier to understand for everyday consumers. Viewing into how factors such as screen size, screen technology, and how much power a television uses.

What we investigate

This project investigates the television market in Australia, including screen technologies available (LCD, LED, and OLED), screen sizes, leading brands, and how said factors relate to power consumption and customer ratings.

Project information

This website was created by Kheldy Nathanael for Task 1(a) of COS30045 at Swinburne University of Technology located at Sarawak, Kuching. The research draws on a dataset of television listings in the Australian market, analyzed to identify patterns seen in televisions.
